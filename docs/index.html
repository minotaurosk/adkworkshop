About

ADK Workshop is the studio of Koray Korkusuz, a self-taught maker who has been turning wood, clay, resin and paper into finished pieces for more than fifteen years. No formal training in painting or sculpture: just curiosity, scrap material and a lot of trial and error.

It started with pencil drawings, polymer clay dragons and painted miniatures in the late 2000s. From 2014 to 2017 it became Atölye Siga, a small artisan workshop in Sığacık, Seferihisar, where olive-wood spoons and bowls, jewelry, sculptures, signs, toys and furniture were made by hand, from the first sketch to the final coat.

Alongside the workshop runs a twenty-year career in IT infrastructure, on projects in Qatar, Azerbaijan and Saudi Arabia. Today the two worlds meet: 3D design and printing, AI-assisted concept art, and tools for makers such as 3D Vault.
